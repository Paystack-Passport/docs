Each country and payment method has a minimum and maximum amount **per transaction**, in the
local currency.

- **Pay-in** is what a payer can send in an [onramp](/api-reference/onramps/execute-an-onramp).
- **Payout** is what a beneficiary can receive from an [offramp](/api-reference/offramps/execute-an-offramp).
- **—** means that direction isn't offered for the country.

**USDC:** the minimum is 2.50 USDC, with no fixed maximum.

## Outside the limits

- An onramp or offramp quote for an amount outside the range is rejected with a `400` error.
- An [auto-conversion](/guides/auto-conversion#minimum-conversion-amount) deposit below the minimum
  isn't rejected. It waits on the credential, and converts with later deposits once the balance
  clears the minimum.
- Same-currency [transfers](/api-reference/transfers/execute-a-transfer) have their own limits,
  set per currency and network.

<Note>
  Limits can change. Last updated 27 October 2026.
</Note>
